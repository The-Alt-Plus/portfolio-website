The Alt Plus is a multidisciplinary studio bringing together architecture, design, art, research, media, technology, and education[01]. Its focus is building systems that navigate across these disciplines to materialize objects with multiple layers of meaning—each directly connected to its generative argument and underlying data[02].

Leveraging computation, digital fabrication, and teaching[03], the studio moves fluidly across scales—from sonic wearables and sculptural installations to acoustic architecture, circular 3D-scanned material archives, planetary environmental research, and international master-level studios and workshops across Europe and the United States.
