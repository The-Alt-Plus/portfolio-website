Practice

SYS // MANIFEST

Design experiences, data, and sound shaped through digital fabrication.

The Alt Plus is the experimental counterpart to Tomás Mena's industrial computational engineering practice. At its core is a commitment to turning intangible signals—environmental data, acoustic frequencies, and spatial narratives—into tangible physical artifacts through digital fabrication (CNC milling, 3D printing, and computational prototyping).

Its output moves fluidly across scales: from digitally fabricated sonic wearables and sculptural installations, to acoustic architecture competitions, circular 3D-scanned material archives, and master-level architecture studios across Europe and the United States.
